DEKCRAFT v0.6.0 — Arc / Chrome / Edge desktop

1. Extract dekcraft-extension.zip.
2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions (Edge: edge://extensions).
3. Enable Developer mode.
4. Choose Load unpacked and select the extracted dekcraft-extension folder.
5. Open YouTube, or refresh a YouTube tab that was already open.
6. Click Settings in the floating Dekcraft toolbar to edit your interests.

If you tried the web demo, export your preferences there, then import that
JSON file from Settings in the extension. The web app does not automatically
connect to the extension.

The extension filters recommendation cards on YouTube Home and Watch pages.
It leaves search, subscriptions, playlists and the video player alone.
Click Check this page in the floating Dekcraft toolbar to flag loaded cards.
Each flagged card shows why it was flagged. Nothing is hidden by a check.
Click Block flagged videos to hide the reviewed cards, or Block this video on
one flagged card. Undo block restores manually blocked cards. Show hidden
reveals hidden cards temporarily. You can pause at any time.

New or changed cards wait for another check. Changing preferences, navigation,
or refresh clears the manual check and page-local blocks. Manual checks flag
all off-topic picks, including discovery. They do not submit YouTube feedback.

For continuous filtering with no off-topic discovery, click Only my interests
in the YouTube toolbar. The button shows On or Off. Click it again to restore
your previous filtering settings, including after a reload. If you are updating
from an older version with no saved prior settings, Off stops automatic filtering.
Turning it on enables Auto-filter, Strict mode and 0% discovery
while preserving your interests and avoid list. The version is shown beside
Dekcraft, and any cards kept for discovery are counted separately.

To keep your existing discovery allowance, click Auto-filter: Off in the YouTube
toolbar so it reads Auto-filter: On. This is also available under Settings >
Auto-filter as you browse. It starts off to preserve manual checking. New and
recycled cards are filtered as their titles load, including during scrolling.
The toolbar shows how many cards are read and hidden. Your discovery budget still
applies; choose Strict and 0% discovery to exclude all off-topic matches.
0% discovery now disables off-topic allowances in every mode.
Undo block does not disable automatic filtering. Show hidden suspends hiding
until you click Hide again; its status is displayed in the toolbar.

Click Hide widget to remove the floating controls. Filtering continues in the
background. Restore them from Arc’s Extensions menu by clicking Dekcraft (or
its pinned extension icon). Visibility is remembered across YouTube reloads.
On unsupported pages the extension icon opens Settings instead.

To update an existing unpacked installation, replace its folder contents with
this release, press Reload on the browser's extensions page, and refresh your
YouTube tabs. Keep the same folder path to retain the extension's preferences.

Semantic blocking (new in v0.4): open Settings > Avoid list, add a broad topic
such as food, and leave Match related topics on. Test title can verify examples
before browsing. The local MiniLM model can relate restaurants and pizza to
food even when that exact word is missing. Sensitivity is adjustable; lower is
broader. Matching is approximate, especially for ambiguous or non-English titles.
Show hidden reveals cards for review, including semantic match explanations.

The model and WebAssembly runtime are included in this extension folder. No
separate model download or AI server is used. The offscreen permission runs a
shared worker away from the YouTube page. New titles are processed in small
batches; the first check takes longer while the model starts. The toolbar shows
checking meanings, semantic ready, or a failure with a retry button. Exact
keyword rules keep working if semantic matching fails. No titles or embeddings
are stored as browsing history. The cache is held only in memory.

Semantic interests (new in v0.5): Settings > My intent > Keep related videos
is on by default, including when updating from an older release. It compares
new titles with your interest names and the first 12 comma-separated keyword
examples per interest. Exact words still match immediately. For AI research,
use examples such as machine learning and neural networks; for mathematics,
linear algebra and geometry; for programming, coding and software. The model
can keep backpropagation, eigenvectors and compiler videos without those exact
keywords. These are semantic examples, not an exhaustive word list.

Use Test title to inspect interest matches. Interest match sensitivity is
separate from avoid-list sensitivity. Zero-priority interests are excluded.
Avoided topics and channels always override interests. Strict mode and 0%
discovery still keep semantic interest matches, including related Shorts.
Automatic filtering evaluates newly loaded titles while the widget is hidden.
Manual Check waits for both interest and avoid matching before flagging cards.
If the model fails, keyword matching remains active and the toolbar shows a
retry button; some related videos may be missed until the model is available.

Edit your names and keyword examples under My intent. Weights choose between matching topics; they do not enforce
a feed quota or change YouTube's own recommendations. An unrelated Short is
filtered when Hide unrelated Shorts is enabled; matching Shorts are retained.

Drift reminders use the last five recommendation clicks, after at least three
selections, with a five-minute reminder cooldown. This is not watch history or
watch-time tracking. Events are temporary and cleared when the browser closes.
Titles, video IDs, and URLs are not stored in session insights.

This is an early MVP. YouTube markup changes and may differ between accounts;
unknown card layouts are left untouched. Keyword matching can miss context.
It has been tested with representative card fixtures; live logged-in YouTube
filtering requires verification in your browser. No Chrome Web Store release
has been created. This is an unpacked installation.

No account, analytics, remote inference, or paid API is used. Preferences stay
in chrome.storage.local. The extension asks for storage, YouTube access, and an offscreen document for
the local model worker.
It never clicks Not interested or other platform feedback controls for you.

To uninstall, remove Dekcraft from the browser extensions page. Your original
YouTube feed returns on refresh. Export preferences first if you want to keep
them. Source is included under the MIT license; fonts use the SIL OFL license.
