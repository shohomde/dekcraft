YOUR FEED. YOUR DATA.

A little clarity
about privacy.

Effective November 12, 2026. Dekcraft is an open-source browser extension and companion website maintained by Shohom De.

What the extension reads

On supported YouTube Home and Watch pages, Dekcraft reads recommendation titles, channel names, card links and whether a card is a Short. It uses that information to apply your filtering rules locally. It does not access your YouTube password, account credentials, video audio or private messages.

Local matching and storage

Keyword matching and semantic inference run on your device. The quantized MiniLM model and its runtime are bundled with the extension. Titles and embeddings are temporarily cached in memory for matching; they are not sent to an AI service or stored as browsing history.

Your interests, avoided topics, blocked channel names, filtering settings and widget visibility are saved in the browser’s extension-local storage. Optional drift reminders use temporary session events containing the matched topic, an alignment flag and a timestamp, capped at 100 events. Those events do not contain video titles, video IDs, URLs or watch-time history.

Permissions and purpose

  • YouTube access: read and hide recommendation cards on www.youtube.com.
  • Storage: remember your preferences and keep temporary session summaries.
  • Offscreen document: run the bundled semantic model in a local worker.

Dekcraft does not submit “Not interested” feedback to YouTube, sell your data, include advertising trackers, or run remote extension code.

The website and playground

The landing page includes no analytics or tracking cookies. The playground stores preferences in your browser’s local storage. It works with illustrative sample recommendations, not your live YouTube feed. Model files load from this website, then matching runs in your browser. The title tester does not upload the title you enter.

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub receives ordinary requests for pages, downloads, fonts and model assets, and may retain service logs under the GitHub privacy statement. Following an external link takes you to a service governed by its own policies.

Your controls

Pause filtering or show hidden cards at any time. Use Settings to edit or reset preferences, or export them to a JSON file you control. Clear this website’s browser storage to remove its saved preferences. Removing the extension removes its local extension data; exported files remain wherever you saved them.

Questions or changes

Report a privacy issue through the Dekcraft repository. Do not include private browsing details in a public issue. Material changes to this page will be recorded in the repository history and reflected in the effective date above.
